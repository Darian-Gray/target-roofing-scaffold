Privacy Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Target Roofers, LLC (“Target Roofers,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards personal information when you visit or make a purchase through our website at www.targetroofers.com (the “Website”). By accessing or using the Website, you consent to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Website.

Information Collection

  • When you fill out a form, register for an account, request a quote, sign up for marketing, or otherwise contact us, we may collect your name, address, email address, phone number, and any other information you choose to provide.

Information Collected Automatically

  • We use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar technologies to gather information such as your IP address, browser and operating system details, referring website, pages visited, and how you interact with the Website.
  • You can disable most cookies via your browser settings; however, parts of the Website may not function properly without them.

Information from Advertising and Analytics Partners

  • We work with third-party partners (e.g., Google Analytics, Constant Contact) who may collect information about your visits on our behalf. We do not receive personal identifiers from those partners unless you have provided consent.

Information from Email and Web Forms

  • Transmission of email sent to us via our web forms is secured by industry-standard SSL encryption. We may retain the content of submitted forms or emails and our responses. Inquiries you submit may be used internally to improve our services or, with your consent, for marketing.

Search Queries and Server Logs

  • If you use any search feature on our site, we may log your query (disassociated from personal identifiers) to improve functionality.
  • Our servers automatically log your activity including the pages you visit, time and date stamps, and other diagnostic data for system administration and security purposes.

Use of Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services and Website functionality

We disclose your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: To third parties who perform services on our behalf (e.g., hosting, marketing, data analysis), provided they agree to keep your data confidential.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties for their own marketing purposes. Non-personally identifiable information may be shared with advertisers or analytics partners.
